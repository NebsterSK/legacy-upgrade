# Post review checklist

Run every draft through this before calling it done. Any **hard fail** blocks publishing.

## Hard fails

- [ ] Contains `-`, `–`, or `—` used as punctuation inside the post copy
- [ ] Contains hashtags
- [ ] More than 3 emojis, or emojis stacked together
- [ ] Missing the `---` + signature closing line
- [ ] Signature line doesn't connect to the post's topic
- [ ] Invented client, metric, or anecdote that didn't actually happen
- [ ] Engagement bait ("Like if you agree", "Comment YES")
- [ ] Named a client without permission

## Hook

- [ ] First line survives the scroll on its own, with the rest collapsed
- [ ] No generic opener ("In today's fast-paced world", "Let's be honest", "Here's the thing")
- [ ] Doesn't open by announcing the topic ("Today I want to talk about automation")

## Substance

- [ ] Exactly one idea. Nothing crammed in.
- [ ] Concrete, not abstract. Specific scenario, number, or situation the reader recognizes.
- [ ] A non-technical business owner understands every sentence
- [ ] Shows expertise through an example, not a claim about himself
- [ ] Not self-congratulatory
- [ ] Doesn't run down competitors or other technologies

## Form

- [ ] Paragraphs are 1–2 sentences
- [ ] 100–250 words, unless the story genuinely needs more
- [ ] Reads as Lukáš in first person, conversational, direct
- [ ] Closes with a soft CTA or real question, not a pitch

## Freshness

- [ ] Angle not used in the last 5 posts
- [ ] Opening move differs from the previous post's
- [ ] Pain point differs from the last 3 posts
- [ ] Post *shape* differs from the previous one (story vs opinion vs myth-bust vs behind-the-scenes)

## The last question

Would a business owner who has never heard of Lukáš get *anything* out of reading this, even if they never hire him? If no, the post is about him instead of about them. Rewrite.
