# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses in Slovakia and the wider CEE region, who
run on spreadsheets, retyped orders and a patchwork of tools that don't talk to each other. They
arrive from a LinkedIn post or a colleague's link, usually at a desk during the working day,
skeptical and short on time. The job: decide in a minute or two whether this person is credible
enough to spend a free consultation call on.

A secondary audience is other developers, who arrive from the developer-focused LinkedIn posts and
judge the site as a piece of craft.

## Product Purpose

A one-page shop window for a freelance developer who builds custom software, automation and
integrations for businesses. Success is a contact: an email, a call, a WhatsApp message. Every
section exists to make that first message feel low-risk (the consultation is free and without
commitment) and worth sending (the work is real, the clients are named, the prices are public).

## Brand Personality

Blunt, dependable, dry. The copy says "No. Well... maybe. Yes, but it will cost you extra." and
"I prefer to make GOOD websites". It is a tradesperson's voice, not an agency's: says what it does,
names the price, refuses what it won't do. Emotional goal: the relief of dealing with someone
straightforward.

## Anti-references

- Wix / Webflow / WordPress template agency sites: stock hero, gradient blobs, "we craft digital
  experiences". The FAQ literally refuses to work on these platforms.
- SaaS-cream landing pages and the editorial-typographic lane (italic serif, ruled columns, mono
  labels). This is a trade, not a magazine.
- Terminal-black developer portfolios with green mono text. The buyer is a business owner, not a
  developer; costume reads as costume.
- "AI agency" purple-blue gradients and glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site is a sample of the work: fast, light, no layout shift,
   nothing that breaks. A slow or fussy page undercuts every claim on it.
2. **The contact is the product.** Every section ends pointing at a way to get in touch; the
   contact section is the finale, not the footer's neighbour.
3. **Blunt beats polished.** Show prices, named clients and real refusals plainly. Visual
   confidence comes from commitment (one colour, big type), not decoration.
4. **The upgrade arrow is the brand.** The blue square with the white up-arrow is the only
   brand asset with equity; build the visual system out of it rather than beside it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard path through header, sections, the FAQ accordion and footer. Visible
focus on every interactive element. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all entrance motion. Content
must be present in the static HTML (the site is a static export and is read by crawlers), so
nothing is gated behind JavaScript.
