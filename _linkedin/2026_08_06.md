For a long time I have been thinking about definition of vibe coding. 🤔

It is not about how much of the code the AI wrote. That question leads nowhere, because for most of us developers the honest answer is already "most of it". So I ended up with three questions instead. That is how it sits in my head.

✅ Three times "yes", and you are doing proper software development, just faster. AI is just a tool you are using well.

⚠️ One single "no" anywhere in that chain, and you are vibe coding. Not because you broke some rule, but because there is nobody left who can answer for that code when it breaks at an inconvenient moment. And it will break at an inconvenient moment.

For a weekend project that is completely fine, and I do it myself. For something a business actually runs on, it is the difference between an asset and a liability that nobody has opened yet.

The tool is the same. The difference is whether you can still take responsibility for what it produced.
