Three days ago, The Blood of Dawnwalker was not getting a 60 FPS mode. Today it is. How is that possible?

The game releases on 3.9.2026. Last Friday the developers put out a gameplay video to show how it runs on consoles. Honest move, but it backfired.

Why? No 60 FPS performance mode. Players (customers) do not just want 60 FPS. They EXPECT it.

💸 What happened then? Backlash. Cancelled preorders.

Today, 3 days later, the developers confirmed a 60 FPS mode in the day one patch. Not "we hear you". Not "we are investigating". Implemented, tested and shipping with the game.

💡 So it was possible the whole time. It just was not a priority until it started costing them money.

I think about this every time a business owner tells me their developer said something is not possible. Sometimes that is completely true and there are real technical reasons behind it. Often it means it was never important enough to anyone, and nobody has had to say that part out loud.

⏱️ Three days of angry customers turned an impossible feature into a finished one.

So if you have been told for years that your system cannot do the thing you need, get a second opinion before you accept it.
