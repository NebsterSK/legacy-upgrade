I am a backend developer. My frontend design instincts are average on a good day.

So I watch two YouTube channels about design, Cole Jain and Sajid. Landing pages, typography, spacing, when an icon helps and when it is noise, how a person actually moves through a page.

It works. I get better. Slowly.

Then something started bothering me. I am learning from these two every week, and the tool doing half of my frontend work has never heard of them.

🎬 You cannot hand a video to an AI, but you can hand it a transcript.

So I pulled the captions from the top 10 videos of each channel with yt-dlp, gave it to Claude, and asked it to find the principles that kept coming back. Not to summarize the videos. To find what they actually agree on. That became a frontend-design skill. 

💡 The part worth stealing is not the CLI command. It is that my AI was missing the exact input that was making me better. The format was the only thing standing in the way.

🎨 I am not suddenly a designer. But the first version of a layout now starts somewhere reasonable, instead of somewhere I have to undo.

And if you write frontend, go watch them yourself. The skill is a shortcut for my tooling, not for me.
